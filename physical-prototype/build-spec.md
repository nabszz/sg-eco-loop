# Build Specification

Prototype-stage dimensions and materials for a child-height demo unit.
All figures are estimates for a first prototype.

## 1. Overall dimensions
| Property | Value | Notes |
|----------|-------|-------|
| Total height | ~1200 mm | Screen centred near a child's eye line (kids ~6–10 yrs) |
| Total width | ~1500 mm | 5 bins + rinsing bay in a row |
| Total depth | ~600 mm | Enough for bin volume + drawer travel |
| Screen | 21–24" touchscreen | Mounted in the curved hood |
| Screen height (centre) | ~1050 mm | Reachable + visible for children |
| Weight (empty) | ~60–80 kg | Ballasted base for stability |

## 2. Zones
### 2.1 Hood + screen (top)
- Curved shell over the top, matching the sketch's rounded hood.
- Houses the touchscreen, controller PC/SBC, and speaker.
- Slight downward tilt (~10–15°) so children look at it comfortably.

### 2.2 Bin row (middle) — 5 identical stations
Per station (each ~250 mm wide):
- **Front push-slot** (flap door) sized to the material:
  - Plastic / Metal: ~180 × 120 mm
  - Paper: ~220 × 60 mm (wide letterbox for flattened paper/card)
  - Glass: ~160 × 160 mm (rounded, padded lip to reduce breakage noise)
  - E-waste: ~150 × 120 mm, lockable (adult-assisted)
- **Clear window** on the front to see fill level (the ~ waves in the sketch).
- **Motorised flap** that only opens after the screen "open bin" step.
- **Removable inner bin / pull-out drawer** for staff to empty.
- **Motion sensor** mounted underneath the slot (child-presence detection).
- **Load cell** under the inner bin to weigh accepted items (reward calc).

### 2.3 Rinsing bay (right)
- Small stainless basin, low-flow tap, soap dispenser.
- Greywater catch tank underneath (no plumbing needed for demo) OR tap into
  mains + drain for a permanent install.
- Splash guard and non-slip mat area.
- Motion sensor to trigger the tap ("space = water" in the sketch).

### 2.4 Base
- Ballasted, low centre of gravity.
- Lockable castors for repositioning at events.
- Cable entry + service access panel at the back.

## 3. Materials
| Part | Material | Reason |
|------|----------|--------|
| Outer shell / hood | Powder-coated aluminium or ABS panels | Light, durable, cleanable |
| Bin row frame | 20×20 mm aluminium extrusion (e.g. T-slot) | Easy prototyping + rigid |
| Front windows | Polycarbonate (not glass) | Child-safe, shatter-resistant |
| Slot flaps | ABS/acrylic with rubber trim | Quiet, no sharp edges |
| Inner bins | HDPE tubs | Cheap, washable, food-safe |
| Rinsing basin | Stainless steel | Hygienic, water-resistant |
| Base ballast | Concrete pavers / steel plate | Stability |

## 4. Child-safety requirements
- All edges rounded (radius ≥ 3 mm); no pinch points on motorised flaps.
- Flap close force limited + obstruction detection (reverses on resistance).
- E-waste bay locked; adult/staff PIN or key to open for collection.
- Electrical enclosure IP-rated and separated from the water bay.
- Anti-tip: unit must not tip when a child leans/pulls on a drawer.
- Water bay: low voltage only nearby, GFCI/RCD protected, no standing water.

## 5. Accessibility
- Screen and slots reachable from a wheelchair where possible (lower slot row).
- High-contrast UI, large touch targets, audio narration of on-screen steps.
